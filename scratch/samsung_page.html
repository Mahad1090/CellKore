FAQs & Tips When Selling Your Samsung Galaxy Phone Online

How do I sell my Samsung Galaxy phone online?

Selling or trading in your Samsung Galaxy online with SmartphonesPLUS is easy and takes just a few minutes. Simply select your Samsung Galaxy’s model and get an instant quote. Then, package your device and ship it to us for free. We’ll send your payout to your selected payment method.

What model of Samsung Galaxy do I have?

It’s important to know exactly what Samsung Galaxy model you have so that you can receive an accurate quote. The quickest way to do this is by going to Settings > About phone or About device.

How fast do I get paid for my Samsung Galaxy phone?

Your payment will be sent after we receive and inspect your device(s), which typically takes between 2-3 business days. We send payment the same day your order is completed.

When should I sell my Samsung Galaxy phone?

The best time to sell your Samsung Galaxy is now. Samsung Galaxy prices tend to drop rather quickly due to decreases in demand and new phone releases.

Our pricing algorithm is built to allow you to get the best price for your phone. It analyzes thousands of prices in the phone resale market so that you’ll receive the most accurate price. However, resale prices for phones can decrease on a weekly or even daily basis which is why you should not wait to sell your Samsung Galaxy.

How do I prepare my Samsung Galaxy for sale?

  1. Sign out and remove your Samsung and Google accounts and all passwords. Please note: We do not accept devices that are account locked or reported as lost or stolen.
  2. Deactivate carrier service & pay off any remaining balance on your account. We may send you a revised offer or have to return your device if this has not been completed.
  3. Backup files if necessary, then factory reset your device(s).
  4. Remember to remove your SD and/or SIM card from your device.
  5. Before shipping, we recommend that you insure your shipment against loss, theft, and damage.
